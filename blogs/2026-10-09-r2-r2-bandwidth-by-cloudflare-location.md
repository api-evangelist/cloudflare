You can now view R2 bandwidth by the Cloudflare location that served each request in the Cloudflare UI. This helps you see which locations consume the most bandwidth with options to select a specific bucket and download (read) vs upload (write) bandwidth. Go to R2 overview ↗ By default, the chart shows the top five locations by total bandwidth consumed during the selected time range.
