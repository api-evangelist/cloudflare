This release introduces new detections to mitigate recent vulnerabilities affecting TanStack and Atlassian products. Key Findings CVE-2026-102989: A reflected Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) vulnerability affecting TanStack. Unauthenticated attackers can exploit this flaw to inject malicious scripts into web pages viewed by other users, potentially leading to session hijacking or unauthorized actions.
