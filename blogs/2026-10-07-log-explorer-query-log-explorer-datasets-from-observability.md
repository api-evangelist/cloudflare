Log Explorer datasets are now queried from the Logs page under Observability in the Cloudflare dashboard. The Logs page brings Log Explorer and Workers Observability datasets together with a shared filter builder, SQL editor, and visualizations. As part of this change, the Log Explorer menu is no longer shown in the dashboard navigation.
