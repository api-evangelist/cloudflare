Cloudflare has improved how it handles and reports client-cancelled HTTP/3 requests across Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans. Customers now get a clearer view of client behavior in Cloudflare analytics and, where available, logs. Previously, Cloudflare did not always stop an HTTP/3 request when the client cancelled its request stream.
