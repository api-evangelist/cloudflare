@cf/daily-co/phonellm-mini-alpha-2 , a model from the Pipecat ↗︎ team at Daily, is now available on Workers AI . PhoneLLM is built for voice agents and other realtime AI applications, where a model has to reply quickly and call the right tool mid-conversation. In a voice agent, the language model sits between speech-to-text and text-to-speech, so every second it takes adds to the caller's wait.
