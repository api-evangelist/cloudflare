Enterprise customers can now configure a zone's CDN Maximum Upload Size up to 5 GB directly from the Network page in the Cloudflare dashboard. This removes the need to contact your account team or Cloudflare Support when applications need to accept request bodies larger than 500 MB and no greater than 5 GB. The default maximum upload size remains 500 MB.
