Cloudflare Log Explorer customers can now permanently delete account and zone datasets from the Cloudflare dashboard or API. Deletion protection is enabled by default to prevent accidental data loss. In the dashboard, go to Manage datasets , disable deletion protection for the dataset, select Delete , and enter the dataset name to confirm.
