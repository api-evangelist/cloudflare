Data loss prevention (DLP) custom pattern entries can use Cloudflare Workers in your account to validate matches with organization-specific logic. Your Worker can adjust a match's confidence or reject the match. For setup steps and the Worker contract, refer to Validate data loss prevention matches with Cloudflare Workers .
