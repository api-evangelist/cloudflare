Passive Detection for Cloudflare Data Loss Prevention (DLP) lets you learn from your Gateway traffic before deciding what to log or block. Discover the sensitive data types in sampled traffic, explore their destinations, and use the findings to build policies around your organization's needs. The dashboard brings together detections from sampled HTTP request and response bodies.
