AI Gateway User Insights now gives you more context about the traffic flowing through your gateway. It shows what users and agents are doing with AI, and where a selected model may be more capable than a task requires. On the analysis side, User Insights groups conversations by task, tracks conversation turns, and helps you compare model fit with cost and latency.
