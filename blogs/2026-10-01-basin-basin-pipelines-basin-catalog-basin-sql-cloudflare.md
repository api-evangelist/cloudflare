Basin , formerly the Cloudflare Data Platform, is now generally available. Basin brings an end-to-end analytics platform to the Developer Platform, enabling you to collect data from a variety of sources, such as apps, infrastructure, devices, and other Cloudflare services, then query it to answer analytical questions. Basin Pipelines Basin Pipelines , formerly Cloudflare Pipelines, ingests events from Workers, HTTP endpoints, and Cloudflare Logpush .
