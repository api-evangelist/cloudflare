The Agents SDK now supports OpenCode 2.0 ↗︎ through the experimental OpenCodeHarness . Run OpenCode inside a plain Durable Object, with sessions stored in SQLite and Lifecycle jobs that wake interrupted work after eviction. The agents/models/opencode provider connects OpenCode to Workers AI through the AI binding.
